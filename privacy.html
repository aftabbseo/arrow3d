Arrow 3D Escape Puzzle Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 November 2026

Who we are

Arrow 3D Escape Puzzle does not ask for your name, email or any account. The game itself keeps your progress only on your device. Our advertising and analytics partners, Google AdMob and Google Firebase Analytics, collect some device data, as described below.

This policy covers the mobile game Arrow 3D Escape Puzzle (Android package com.pixelwinlabs.arrow3d), made by PixelWin Labs ("we", "us"). By using the game you agree to this policy.

Information we collect

We collect no personal information you type in. The game has no sign-in, chat or in-app purchases with real money.

We do not collect your name, email, phone number, contacts, photos, or precise GPS location. The game asks for no device permissions beyond internet access and vibration.

How we use information

We use the data only to run and improve Arrow 3D Escape Puzzle:

  • Save your progress on your device
  • Show ads, including rewarded ads you choose to watch for coins, hearts or hints
  • Find levels that are too hard or too easy, and fix bugs
  • Count how many people play, and for how long

We do not sell your personal information. We do not use it to identify you personally.

Your choices

You control how your data is used for ads:

  • Consent popup. In the EEA, UK and Switzerland, and other places where the law requires it, the game asks for your consent before ads load.
  • Privacy Choices. Where consent applies, open Settings in the game and tap Privacy Choices to change your answer at any time.
  • Personalised ads. On Android, open Settings > Google > Ads to delete or reset your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads.
  • Stop all collection. Uninstall the game. Uninstalling also deletes the progress saved on your device.

Children

Arrow 3D Escape Puzzle is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child under 13 has given us data, contact us and we will delete what we can.

How long data is kept

Progress on your device stays until you uninstall the game. Firebase Analytics keeps event data for no more than 14 months. Google keeps ad data under its own policy.

Security

Data sent to Google travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS). No method of storage or transfer is completely secure, but we use the protections these services provide.

International transfers

Google may process data in the United States and other countries. Google uses legal safeguards, such as Standard Contractual Clauses, for these transfers.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in the EU and UK, or the CCPA in California), you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal data held about you
  • Correct or delete it
  • Object to or limit how it is used, including for personalised ads
  • Withdraw consent at any time

We do not hold data that links your play to your name, so most requests are best made through the choices above or directly with Google. You can also contact us, and we will help.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the game changes. The date at the top shows the latest version. Big changes will also be noted in the game or its store listing.
